4Daily calorie targets每日熱量目標（可留空）
General adult reference: about 2000 kcal/day for women, 2500 for men. These are common general figures — actual needs vary with age, size and activity. This is not personalised dietary advice.
一般成人參考：女約 2000、男約 2500 kcal／日。實際因人而異，此非個人化建議。
How it works 點運作：everyone eats the same dishes. The system writes a per-person portion adjustment (skip the rice, half the sauce, sauce on the side) instead of cooking separate meals. Calorie figures are estimates shown as ranges.
Flavour never gets sacrificed: the engine is instructed that reducing calories by cutting seasoning, spices or steps is a failure — it must use technique instead (less sugar, steam or roast instead of deep-fry, leaner cuts, sauce served separately, more umami to compensate). Every dish keeps its chef's tip.
全家食同一枱菜，只出個人份量調整。味道唔會為熱量讓步——每道菜必定保留提升貼士。
Leave empty to disable entirely. Targets below 1200 kcal/day are refused by the system.（留空即不啟用。低於 1200 kcal／日系統會拒絕。）
