The product is the loop

Most meal planners are a pretty page you abandon in three weeks.

This one closes the circle. Each station feeds the next, and the last one feeds the first — which is the only reason it's still running in month six.

STATION 01

Projection

The system knows what you bought and what the week's menu will consume. It projects what's actually left in the fridge — before you open it.

STATION 02

Shopping list

The gap between the projection and the menu is the list. Nobody writes it. It arrives already grouped by where you buy things.

STATION 03

Morning brief

What to thaw, what to prep, what to cook, in the reader's own language. One message, on the phone already in the kitchen.

STATION 04

Ratings

One tap after dinner. Dishes your family actually likes come back sooner. Dishes that flopped quietly stop appearing.

Ratings reshape next week's menu — which changes the projection. Back to 01.

Honest limits

Things that would annoy you on day one, said on day zero.

You're going to find these out anyway. Better here than after you've paid.

Setup time
First deployment takes about 45 minutes. It is not one-click-and-done. If that's a dealbreaker, wait for the app.
Support
Documentation only. No one-to-one setup help. The manual and the FAQ are the support, and they're written to carry that weight.
Refunds
14 days, no questions asked, on both editions.
Running cost
AWS free tier covers a normal household. AI features are optional and use your own API key, so you're never billed through us.
Diet modes
Balanced-by-colour is the default. Mediterranean, keto, high-protein, 16:8 and low-sodium modes adjust one person's portions without changing what the family eats. Calorie figures are shown as ranges and labelled as estimates, because that's what they are.
Allergies
Set them once and the menu planner avoids those ingredients. Treat it as a planning aid, not a safety guarantee — always read the ingredients yourself before serving.
What it isn't
Not medical or dietary advice, and not a weight-loss program. It runs a household kitchen.
