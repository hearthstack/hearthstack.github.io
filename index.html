FAQ

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The Setup Manual is click-by-click with screenshots, and the Setup Wizard generates every setting for you by asking 8 questions. Most households are fully running in about an hour; the Notion layer alone takes 15 minutes.

What does it cost to run?

The engine fits comfortably in the AWS free tier, Notion free plan is enough, Telegram is free, and AI menu drafting uses your own free Gemini key. Typical ongoing cost: $0.

Does it work without an AI key?

Yes — inventory tracking, daily briefs, approvals and ratings all run without AI. You can draft weekly menus yourself, or paste our prompt into any AI chat you already use (manual, Appendix 3).

Where does my family's data live?

In your Notion workspace and your AWS account, full stop. We have no server, no analytics on your data, and no way to see it. That's a design decision, not a promise.

My helper doesn't read Chinese / my partner doesn't read English

That's the point: the Helper receives the morning brief in English, the Partner in Chinese, from the same system. Bahasa and Tagalog packs are on the roadmap.

What if Telegram is blocked or shut down someday?

Your data is in Notion, untouched. The notification layer is a thin swappable adapter — a Discord-webhook fallback guide is maintained on this page.

What support do I get?

This is a self-serve product: the manual, this FAQ and the changelog are the support channel, kept current with every release. If the documentation genuinely fails you, the 14-day refund applies — no questions asked.

Can I modify the code?

Yes, for your own household — it's a clean, zero-dependency, commented codebase with a 64-assertion test suite included. Redistribution or reselling isn't allowed (see LICENSE).
