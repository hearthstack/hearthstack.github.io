Hearthstack Kitchen

Let's set up your kitchen 🏮

Five short steps, about 35 minutes, no coding. Everything installs into your own free accounts — your family's data never touches our servers.
五步，約 35 分鐘，唔使識 code。全部裝入你自己嘅免費帳戶 — 資料唔經我哋。

⏱️ Honest timing: Step 1 alone gives you a working meal-planning workspace in 15 minutes. Steps 2–4 add the automation (briefs, stock alerts, AI menus). Do them in one sitting or come back later — your progress keeps.
💡 One tip before you start: where possible, use the same Google account for Notion sign-in and your AI key — fewer accounts to remember. Different accounts work fine too; just note which one holds what.
盡量用同一個 Google account 登入各樣嘢；唔同都得，記住邊個 account 擺咗乜就好。
1Get your Notion workspace15 min · usable now

Tap the button, then tap Duplicate (top-right). That's your whole kitchen database — menus, pantry, recipes — with sample data so every screen makes sense. All 87 recipes ship in four languages — 中文, English, Tagalog and Bahasa Indonesia.

撳掣 → 右上角 Duplicate。成套廚房系統連示範資料即刻屬於你。

📄 Duplicate the template

Needs: a free Notion account (it'll prompt you).

Then connect it (2 taps — needed for Step 4)

1. Go to notion.so/profile/integrations → New integration → name it hearthstack → Save → copy the secret (starts with ntn_).
2. Open your duplicated page → ••• (top-right) → Connections → connect hearthstack. Done — keep the secret for Step 3.

2Create your family's bot3 min

Tap to open @BotFather in Telegram → send /newbot → give it a name (e.g. Our Kitchen) and a username ending in bot. Copy the token it replies with.

開 BotFather → /newbot → 改名 → copy 佢回覆嘅 token。

🤖 Open BotFather

Needs: Telegram (which your family will use daily anyway).

3Launch your engine on AWS10 min

One tap opens AWS with everything pre-configured. Sign in, paste your bot token and Notion secret (and your free AI key if you have one — optional), tick the acknowledgement box, tap Create stack. AWS builds the whole engine for you.

一撳開 AWS（已預設好晒）→ 登入 → 貼 bot token 同 Notion secret → 撳 Create stack。引擎自動起好。

🚀 Launch on AWS

Needs: an AWS account (free tier covers this system; sign-up asks for a card but typical running cost is $0). Create one here first if you don't have it.

Optional: free AI key for auto menu drafting (5 min)

Go to aistudio.google.com → Get API key → Create → copy. Paste it into the GeminiKey field when launching. Skip it and everything still works — you'll write menus yourself.

What you get for free: one-tap updates 一撳更新

The stack sets an UPDATE_URL pointing at the current engine build. When we ship an improvement, you tap update in the Console and your engine pulls the new version — no reinstall, no re-pasting keys. Your recipes, settings and history stay exactly where they are.
安裝時已設定 UPDATE_URL。有新版時喺 Console 一撳即更新，唔使重裝、唔使再貼 key，食譜同設定原封不動。

When the stack shows CREATE_COMPLETE (2–3 min), open the Outputs tab — you'll see your SetupDoctorUrl and PairingCode. You need both in Step 4.
4Doctor check & pair your family5 min

Open your SetupDoctorUrl (from Outputs). The Doctor checks all seven layers top-down, fixes what it can by itself, and tells you exactly what to tap if anything's red. Then open your bot in Telegram, send your PairingCode — you're the Admin. Family members just send /start and you approve them with one tap.

開 SetupDoctorUrl — 七步自檢，自己修得嘅自己修，紅咗會話你知撳邊度。之後喺 bot send 配對碼成為 Admin，屋企人 /start 你一撳批准。

SetupDoctorUrl → 喺 AWS stack 嘅 Outputs 分頁 copy
5Set allergies & calorie targets2 min · do it once

In your bot, tap 🚫 過敏原 and tick anything that must never appear — crustaceans, molluscs, fish, egg, milk, peanuts, tree nuts, soy, gluten, sesame, sulphites, plus no-pork / no-alcohol / no-beef. Tap 💾 完成. From then on every menu avoids them automatically; you'll never be asked again.

喺 bot 撳 🚫 過敏原 → 剔走絕不可出現嘅項目 → 撳 💾 完成。之後每次出菜單自動避開，唔會再問你。

Optional: tap 🔥 熱量目標 to set a daily calorie target per person. Everyone still eats the same dishes — the system writes a per-person portion adjustment instead of cooking separate meals.

可選：撳 🔥 熱量目標 設定各人每日熱量。全家食同一枱菜，只出個人份量調整。

Please read: the allergy filter is a planning aid, not a safety guarantee. Always read the ingredients yourself before cooking and serving. Sauces hide allergens — oyster sauce contains shellfish, wheat and soy; fish sauce and dashi contain fish; kimchi usually contains shrimp paste.
過敏過濾係編餐輔助，唔係安全保證。落單及煮食前請自行核對成分。
Why flavour never suffers 點解唔會為熱量而變寡味

The engine is instructed that cutting seasoning, spices or steps to save calories is a failure. It must use technique instead — less sugar, no thickening, steam or roast rather than deep-fry, leaner cuts, sauce served on the side, more umami to compensate. Every dish keeps its chef's tip.
引擎明文規定：靠減調味、減香料、省步驟嚟減熱量係不合格，只可用手法達成。每道菜必定保留提升貼士。
